After Kulgam killings, Uttar Pradesh boosts security across Kanwar Yatra routes

A day after two migrant labourers from Chhattisgarh were shot dead by terrorists in Jammu and Kashmir's Kulgam district, the Uttar Pradesh Police intensified security measures across the state, with special focus on Kanwar Yatra routes and the state capital, Lucknow, Indian Express reported.

The heightened security also comes ahead of the Uttar Pradesh Assembly's monsoon session, which is scheduled to begin on Monday.

Police personnel deployed along Kanwar Yatra routes have been instructed to remain on high alert and respond immediately to any suspicious person, object or activity. Field officers have been directed to strengthen surveillance, conduct regular inspections and maintain close coordination to ensure a swift response to any potential security threat.

Senior police officials have also been asked to carry out routine field visits to review security arrangements and ensure that all directions are being implemented effectively, Indian Express reported.

The annual Kanwar Yatra began on Thursday amid elaborate security arrangements across Uttar Pradesh, with lakhs of devotees expected to participate in the pilgrimage.

A day after the yatra commenced, Chief Secretary S.P. Goel and Director General of Police (DGP) Rajeev Krishna reviewed the state's security preparedness and logistical arrangements.

During the review meeting, DGP Krishna instructed officials to act promptly on any information related to suspicious activities and emphasised that no security alert should be ignored. He also directed all agencies to remain vigilant throughout the pilgrimage.

The security review follows the killing of two migrant labourers, identified as Deepak Ratre and Bhupinder Bhaina, who were shot dead at point-blank range by terrorists in South Kashmir's Kulgam district on Friday.

The attack came barely 10 days after a Jammu and Kashmir Police head constable was shot dead by terrorists in a crowded market in Anantnag district. It also marked the first major terror strike in the Union Territory since the April 2025 Pahalgam terror attack, in which 26 civilians were killed, Indian Express reported.
