Vijay’s Rs 80 Crore Chennai home: 6 bedrooms, glass walls, sea view, Olympic-sized pool and private gym

Thalapathy Vijay has spent decades as one of Tamil cinema’s biggest stars, building a massive fan following through blockbuster films and memorable performances. As attention around the actor continues to grow, particularly with Jana Nayagan generating buzz, fans have also shown renewed curiosity about his life beyond the big screen. One part of Vijay’s private world that frequently attracts interest is his luxurious Chennai residence, reportedly worth between Rs 70 crore and Rs 80 crore.

Located in the upscale Neelankarai neighbourhood along Chennai’s East Coast Road, Vijay’s home reportedly sits on Casuarina Drive and overlooks the Bay of Bengal. The beachfront location, contemporary architecture and emphasis on privacy make the property stand out among celebrity homes in the city.

Modern Beachfront Architecture

Instead of opting for excessive ornamentation, Vijay’s residence reportedly follows a modern and minimalist design approach. Its clean architectural lines and open spaces have led to comparisons with luxurious beachfront villas seen along the California coast.



One of its most striking features is said to be the glass-fronted façade. Large glass surfaces and floor-to-ceiling windows allow plenty of natural light into the house while offering expansive views of the coastline.

Reports suggest Vijay was inspired by modern beachfront homes he encountered during a visit to the United States and wanted a similar open and airy feel for his Chennai residence.

Neutral And Minimalist Interiors

The interiors reportedly continue the minimalist theme seen outside the property. Ivory, beige and natural wood tones dominate the décor, giving the spaces a warm yet understated look.



High ceilings and open-plan living areas reportedly make the house feel spacious, while the neutral colour palette keeps the focus on the natural surroundings and ocean views.

Rather than filling the property with overly elaborate decorative elements, the design appears centred on comfort, functionality and simplicity.



Private Gym And Olympic-Sized Swimming Pool

Vijay’s home reportedly includes several premium amenities, including a private gym. Known for maintaining his fitness throughout his long film career, the actor is said to have a dedicated workout area within the property.

Another standout feature is the reported Olympic-sized swimming pool. Apart from adding to the luxury factor of the residence, the pool offers Vijay a private space where he can exercise and unwind away from public attention.

These features complement the actor’s disciplined lifestyle, particularly considering the physical demands associated with many of his film roles.



Six Bedrooms And Zen-Style Courtyard

The beachfront mansion reportedly has six bedrooms, providing ample space while still maintaining a sense of privacy.

A Zen-style courtyard is another notable feature associated with the property. Combined with landscaped gardens and a peaceful pathway leading towards the entrance, the outdoor spaces reportedly contribute to the home’s calm and secluded atmosphere.

Smart home automation is also said to be incorporated into the residence, bringing modern technology into its minimalist design.



A Private Retreat Away From The Spotlight

Despite Vijay’s enormous popularity, his Chennai residence appears to have been designed primarily as a private family retreat rather than a display of extravagant celebrity wealth.

Its beachfront setting, natural lighting, muted interiors and landscaped surroundings create a balance between luxury and simplicity.

With an estimated value of around Rs 70 crore to Rs 80 crore, the Neelankarai property is undoubtedly an expensive celebrity residence. However, what makes Vijay’s home particularly distinctive is its restrained approach to luxury.

From panoramic sea views and large glass windows to a private gym, swimming pool, six bedrooms and a tranquil courtyard, Vijay’s Chennai home reflects a preference for contemporary comfort while keeping life away from the cameras relatively private.
