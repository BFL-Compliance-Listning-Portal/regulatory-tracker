10 most-searched domestic and international travel destination for Diwali holidays 2026

Diwali is increasingly becoming an opportunity for Indians to turn the festive break into a holiday, with travellers exploring everything from heritage cities and spiritual retreats to international beach destinations and vibrant Asian capitals. This year, Udaipur, Goa and Jaipur are leading domestic travel searches, while Bangkok, Dubai and Tokyo are emerging as the most-searched international destinations for the festive period.

According to the latest search data from Booking.com, Indian travellers are planning getaways between November 6 and November 14, 2026, with a strong preference for domestic leisure destinations and short-haul international holidays.

The findings point towards diverse travel preferences, with couples, families, solo travellers and larger groups choosing destinations based on the kind of festive experience they want.

Best places to visit in India during Diwali 2026

For those planning a domestic holiday this Diwali, Rajasthan is attracting considerable interest, with Udaipur securing the top position in Booking.com's most-searched domestic destinations list. Known for its lakes, palaces and heritage experiences, the city is followed by Goa and Jaipur.

The rankings also highlight the appeal of spiritual tourism, with Varanasi coming in fourth and Puri and Rishikesh featuring among the top seven destinations.

The search patterns reveal interesting differences in traveller preferences. While metropolitan cities such as Mumbai, New Delhi and Bengaluru see stronger searches from couples and solo travellers, heritage destinations such as Udaipur, Jaipur and Varanasi attract a more balanced mix of couples, families and groups.

Nature-led destinations are also finding favour among family travellers. Munnar and Puri, for instance, are popular choices for families and larger groups looking to spend quality time together.

This suggests that the festive holiday is no longer limited to visiting family or returning home. For many, it is also an opportunity to explore a new destination and spend time with loved ones.

Best international destinations for Diwali holidays 2026

For Indians looking to travel overseas, Asia-Pacific destinations dominate the international rankings, accounting for eight of the top 10 most-searched destinations.

Bangkok leads the list, followed by Dubai and Tokyo. Singapore and Phuket complete the top five, highlighting the appeal of destinations that offer a combination of city experiences, beaches, shopping, food and cultural exploration.

One interesting addition to this year's list is Phu Quoc in Vietnam, reflecting the appeal of island destinations among Indian holidaymakers.

While Bangkok, Phuket, Phu Quoc, Hanoi and Hong Kong are attracting couples and groups, Dubai, Tokyo and Singapore are appealing across different traveller categories, particularly families.

London is the only European destination to feature in the top 10, indicating that while long-haul holidays remain relevant, regional international destinations have a strong presence in Indian travellers' festive holiday plans.

Couples, families and solo travellers: How Indians are choosing their Diwali holidays

Beyond the destination rankings, the search data highlights how travel preferences differ across traveller segments.

Couples and solo travellers are driving searches in major metropolitan cities, including Mumbai, New Delhi and Bengaluru. Meanwhile, heritage and spiritual destinations are attracting a broader mix of travellers.

For families and larger groups, nature-focused destinations and relaxing holiday spots are emerging as preferred choices.

Internationally, couples and groups are gravitating towards destinations that offer a mix of nightlife, beaches, culinary experiences and local culture. Families, on the other hand, are showing interest in destinations such as Dubai, Tokyo and Singapore.

These patterns suggest that Indian travellers are increasingly looking at the overall holiday experience when choosing where to spend their festive break.

Making festive holidays more memorable with shared experiences

Reflecting the growing interest in group and family holidays, Booking.com has introduced its limited-period 'Scene Set Hai' campaign, aimed at making festive travel more accessible.

The campaign offers discounts of 15% or more on participating properties across domestic and international destinations. Bookings can be made between September 28 and November 9, 2026, for stays between October 1, 2026, and January 7, 2027.

Commenting on the changing travel preferences of Indian consumers, Santosh Kumar, Regional Manager, South Asia, Booking.com, said: "We're seeing a shift in how Indians approach holiday travel. It's no longer just about taking a break, it's about micro-vacations, spontaneous getaways and discovering unique local cultures together."

He added that younger travellers are exploring offbeat island destinations, while multigenerational families are increasingly considering spacious holiday homes for shared experiences.

The bigger picture: Diwali is becoming an opportunity to travel differently

From Udaipur's lakes and Jaipur's heritage to Bangkok's energetic city life and Tokyo's urban experiences, the 2026 Diwali travel searches highlight the diversity of Indian holiday preferences.

Domestic destinations continue to offer a mix of culture, spirituality and nature, while international travel searches point towards the appeal of accessible Asian getaways.

For travellers still planning their festive break, the rankings offer inspiration across budgets, travel styles and holiday durations. Whether it is a quiet family retreat, a beach escape with friends or an international city holiday, this Diwali presents plenty of possibilities beyond the traditional festive celebrations.
